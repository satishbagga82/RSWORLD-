RS WORLD BUSINESS APP – VERSION 2

Features: Dashboard, leads, calling, interested/not interested/follow-up, sales, installation tracking (appointment date, order/service ID, address, installer), invoice/billing, expenses, installers, reports, backup/import.

WhatsApp: Invoice History and Sales list include WhatsApp message buttons. Buttons open the customer chat in WhatsApp with a pre-filled invoice or sale/installation update. You must press Send yourself. The message will be sent from the WhatsApp account logged in on your phone (your number). Automatic sending requires official WhatsApp Business Platform/API setup and customer opt-in.

To use: Extract ZIP, open index.html in Chrome, menu > Add to Home screen.
Data is stored locally in that browser/device; regularly export JSON backups. This is a prototype and not a certified GST/accounting replacement.
